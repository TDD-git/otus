# Regulatory recurring workflows 2025–2027 (evidence pack)

**Research date:** 2026-07-17  
**Scope:** US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia  
**Method:** ≥15 regulatory changes screened; competitive checks via Shopify App Store, SaaS directories, industry sites/forums; **8 best retained** (no product recommendation).

**Filter applied:** obligation in force or by end-2027; microbusiness/self-employed workflow (not generic legal advice); penalty / licence / market-access stakes; PDF/email/spreadsheet workaround common; SaaS plausible at ~$20–200/mo without licensed legal advice.

## Top 8 (evidence only)

### 1) Australia — Payday Super + SBSCH closure

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Payday Super: employers must pay SG so funds receive it within **7 business days of payday**, from **1 Jul 2026**; calculate on “qualifying earnings”; SBSCH closed **1 Jul 2026**. Official: [ATO About Payday Super](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/about-payday-super); [ATO SBSCH](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/quarterly-super-to-30-june-2026/paying-super-contributions/how-to-pay-super/small-business-superannuation-clearing-house); [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/news/changes-for-businesses-from-1-july-2026) |
| **Official / cited affected** | All employers with SG obligations. Niche repeatedly cited: **~250,000** SBSCH users who lose free clearing house ([CPA Australia](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/public-practice/inpractice/practice-management/what-looming-advent-payday-super-means-sme-employers); [SMSF Adviser on PCG 2026/1](https://www.smsfadviser.com/payday-super-atos-practical-compliance-guideline-pcg-2026-1/)). Full employer population is larger (outside preferred 10k–500k); SBSCH cohort fits. |
| **Recurring workflow** | Every pay run: calculate SG on QE → pay via SuperStream-compliant clearing house → confirm receipt within 7 business days → fix rejected fund payments → monitor SGC risk. Pre-2026 workaround: quarterly SBSCH batch + spreadsheets. |
| **Stakes** | Automatic **super guarantee charge** assessment (no SG statement lodge); late-payment penalties **25%/50%** of unpaid SGC; prior max 200% regime replaced. [ATO SGC](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/missed-or-late-payday-super-payments/the-new-super-guarantee-charge) |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | Integrated: **MYOB Pay Super** (included in payroll subs) [MYOB hub](https://www.myob.com/au/support/myob-business/payday-super-support-hub); **Xero / Employment Hero / KeyPay** payroll+super; commercial clearing houses e.g. **Beam** [beamconnect.com.au](https://beamconnect.com.au/payday-super-compliance/). Not “empty”; gap is micro employers still on SBSCH/spreadsheets without full payroll suite. Funding: incumbents are public/large PE — not greenfield. |
| **Unmet gap (evidence-based)** | WPN-only / edge cases (MYOB notes WPN not yet on Pay Super); employers with multi-fund chaos + cashflow calendar who refuse full accounting suite; migration tooling from SBSCH export → commercial CH with rejection-retry UX. |
| **Distribution** | ATO SuperStream product register; accountant/bookkeeper channels; Xero/MYOB app marketplaces; SME Facebook/tradie forums. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | Federal SG statute; PCG 2026/1 soft-landing year reduces enforcement risk temporarily, not the obligation. Low repeal risk given bipartisan super trajectory. |

### 2) Canada — ITA s.67.7 non-compliant short-term rental expense denial + municipal licence stack

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | *Income Tax Act* **s.67.7** (Bill C-69 Royal Assent **20 Jun 2024**): denies deductions for expenses of **non-compliant** STRs (prohibited locally or missing licence/permit) for expenses after **2023**; 2024-only deemed-compliance if licensed by **31 Dec 2024**. [Justice Laws](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-67.7.html); [CRA tip 2025](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2025/changes-rules-eligible-deductions-short-term-rental-income.html); [CRA sharing economy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/platform-economy/sharing-economy.html) |
| **Official / cited affected** | StatCan/AirDNA: **355,070** STR listings Canada (2023); **107,266** “potential long-term dwellings”. [StatCan 11-621-M](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2024010-eng.htm) (released 2024). Host count < listings; PLTD+licensed-city hosts sit inside ~10k–500k. |
| **Recurring workflow** | Track municipal/provincial licence status + night caps + principal-residence rules → calendar of compliant vs non-compliant days → allocate expenses for T776 → retain permits for CRA (no reassessment time limit under s.67.7(4) per commentary). Workaround: Excel + city portal PDFs + Airbnb messages. |
| **Stakes** | Taxed on **gross** rent for non-compliant days (expense denial); platform delisting; municipal fines; federal deduction denial indefinite reassessment risk ([Thor Tax Blog Feb 2025](https://www.thor.ca/blog/2025/02/income-tax-act-now-denies-deductions-for-non-compliant-short-term-rentals/)). |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | **Permit Ready Pros** — Toronto-focused compliance OS (permits, MAT, 180-night) [permitreadypros.com](https://permitreadypros.com/); **Trackable Doors** — US cities (Austin/Nashville/Scottsdale) [trackabledoors.com](https://www.trackabledoors.com/); PMS **Hostaway/Guesty** store licence fields for Airbnb, not federal tax-day apportionment. National multi-city CA + s.67.7 expense engine appears thin. |
| **Unmet gap** | Cross-Canada rule engine (Toronto/Vancouver/Montreal/Québec + BC STRAA) tied to **day-weighted expense denial** and CRA-ready PDF pack — not another PMS. |
| **Distribution** | Airbnb host forums; Facebook STR Canada groups; municipal licence offices; accountant/T776 season; Hostaway/Guesty integrations. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | Federal tax rule can be amended by Parliament; municipal rules change frequently (product must version rules). Housing-politics risk: further tightening more likely than repeal through 2027. |

### 3) EU — General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) Reg. (EU) 2023/988

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Applicable **13 Dec 2024**. Responsible person in EU; distance-sale offers must show manufacturer + RP contacts + product ID + warnings. Commission notice C/2025/6233 (2025). [EUR-Lex C/2025/6233](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/6233/oj); [Commission factsheet PDF](https://commission.europa.eu/system/files/2023-07/factsheet%20GPSR%20final.pdf); [Shopify Help GPSR](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/gpsr) |
| **Official / cited affected** | All economic operators placing non-food consumer products on EU market (incl. distance sellers). No single official “micro seller” headcount; Shopify/Etsy EU-ship sellers are the practical micro cohort (order-of-magnitude tens–hundreds of thousands globally — treat as **estimate**, not official). |
| **Recurring workflow** | Appoint/maintain EU RP → keep technical docs / risk assessments per SKU → publish listing fields → update labels → respond to marketplace takedowns. Workaround: PDF DoCs emailed to RP + spreadsheet of SKUs + manual theme edits. |
| **Stakes** | Market surveillance; product withdrawal; marketplace suspension; MS penalties (GPSR requires dissuasive penalties; national amounts vary). Shopify community reports listing blocks pending RP. [Shopify Community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-to-be-gpsr-compliant-and-locate-a-responsible-person-in-the-eu/390224) |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | **Shopify listing apps:** GPSReady **$7.99–$14.99/mo** [apps.shopify.com/gpsready](https://apps.shopify.com/gpsready); GPSR Shield free+$9.99/mo [apps.shopify.com/gpsr-app](https://apps.shopify.com/gpsr-app). **RP services:** Eldris **£195** onboard + **£19.95–£249.95/mo** by SKU [eldris.ai/pricing](https://eldris.ai/pricing/); EaseCert **€400–€500** one-time [easecert.com](https://easecert.com/pages/what-is-the-cost-of-a-responsible-person-service-for-eu-compliance). Market **not empty**; display layer crowded; quality/tech-file automation uneven. |
| **Unmet gap** | Continuous SKU change-control (new variants → auto risk-assessment stubs + DoC hash + RP sync) for micro catalogs without consultancy retainers; Etsy-native parity vs Shopify-first apps. |
| **Distribution** | Shopify App Store; Amazon Seller Central / Etsy forums; EU marketplace compliance emails. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | EU regulation; evaluation clauses exist in broader product-safety acquis; low near-term repeal. National enforcement intensity varies (arbitrage risk, not sunset). |

### 4) EU — Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) Reg. (EU) 2025/40

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Entered into force **11 Feb 2025**; general application **12 Aug 2026**. Harmonises packaging EPR registration / producer obligations across MS; distance sellers / non-EU producers called out in secondary commentary. Official: [EC packaging waste page](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/packaging-waste_en); Commission guidance notice C/2026/3084 [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ%3AC_202603084) |
| **Official / cited affected** | All producers placing packaging/packaged products on EU market. No single EU headcount for “micro distance sellers”; practical audience = multi-country Amazon/Shopify shippers already juggling LUCID/Citeo/etc. |
| **Recurring workflow** | Per-MS packaging EPR register → authorised representative where required → weigh/classify packaging by material → annual declarations + eco-modulated fees → marketplace proof-of-registration. Workaround: country-by-country PDF portals + Excel weights. |
| **Stakes** | Cannot legally place packaging without registration (Art. 44/45 architecture per industry guides); marketplace verification / listing blocks ([Lovat](https://vatcompliance.co/blog/ppwr-requirements-2026-eu-directive-packaging-law-epr-registration-and-full-compliance-guide/); [Eldris PPWR](https://epr.eldris.ai/ppwr/)). |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | **Lovat / Lappa** EPR reporting **€190–€620/yr** tiers + registration quotes [vatcompliance.co/prices/epr](https://vatcompliance.co/prices/epr/); [lappa.org/epr-pricing](https://lappa.org/epr-pricing/); LinkedIn notes Lovat ~$24.8k funding (third-party claim). National PROs (LUCID, Citeo, CONAI…) remain fee collectors. Market occupied by VAT/EPR compliance firms; Shopify-native lightweight ops layer thinner. |
| **Unmet gap** | Auto-estimate packaging weights from Shopify SKUs + multi-MS registration status dashboard under **$50–150/mo** without full-service AR retainers; PPWR DoC / labelling calendar separate from legacy EPR. |
| **Distribution** | Amazon EU seller apps; Shopify; VAT/EPR agencies; ecommerce Facebook groups (UK/US sellers into EU). |
| **Sunset / preemption** | Regulation with staged 2030+ design targets; implementing acts still arriving — **scope creep / delay risk**, not repeal. National EPR systems persist underneath. |

### 5) US (California) — SB 54 packaging EPR producer registration

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Plastic Pollution Prevention and Packaging Producer Responsibility Act (SB 54, 2022); permanent regs effective **1 May 2026**; producers must by **1 Jun 2026** join PRO (Circular Action Alliance), register as independent producer, or apply **small producer exemption** (<$1M CA gross sales) via PEPRS. Full program fees **1 Jan 2027**. [CalRecycle Producer Guidance](https://calrecycle.ca.gov/packaging/packaging-epr/producerguidance/); [CalRecycle exemptions](https://calrecycle.ca.gov/packaging/packaging-epr/exemptionsexclusions/); [Mayer Brown May/Jun 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/californias-sb-54-epr-regulations-take-effect-key-deadlines-and-compliance-obligations-for-producers); [CAA news](https://circularactionalliance.org/news-feed/sb-54-regulations-are-final-what-producers-need-to-know-now-n35jy) |
| **Official / cited affected** | “Producers” of single-use packaging / plastic food serviceware sold in CA. Official producer census not published as a single micro count; small-producer exemption still requires PEPRS registration — creates workflow for DTC brands under $1M CA sales. CAA also PRO in OR/CO/MN (multi-state stack). |
| **Recurring workflow** | Classify covered materials → register CAA or PEPRS → submit supply / source-reduction data (CAA cites May 31, 2026 reporting deadlines in 2026 cycle) → pay fees from 2027 → renew exemption attestations. Workaround: spreadsheets + consultant PDFs. |
| **Stakes** | CalRecycle administrative enforcement / penalties for non-registration; market-access via retail/DTC in CA; fee liability from 2027. |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | **CAA Producer Portal** (registration free) [circularactionalliance.org/registration](https://circularactionalliance.org/registration); PEPRS government system; consultancies (H2 Compliance etc.). Dedicated **$20–200/mo SaaS** for DTC SKU→material mapping + multi-state CAA calendar is sparse vs EU Lovat-class tools. |
| **Unmet gap** | Shopify order→packaging material estimator + “am I a producer / small exemption?” wizard + deadline CRM across CA/OR/CO/MN without enterprise EPR consultants. |
| **Distribution** | CalRecycle webinars; CAA Producer Resource Center; CPG/DTC Slack; Shopify plus agencies; Amazon Brand Registry communities. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | State law; federal packaging EPR preemption **not** enacted as of research date — multi-state divergence risk (product must track state matrix). Political repeal risk moderate vs EU regulation. |

### 6) US (California) — Automatic Renewal Law amendments (AB 2863)

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §17600 et seq. as amended by **AB 2863**; operative **1 Jul 2025**. Online cancel “at will” without obstructive steps; simultaneous “click to cancel” if retention offers shown; consent record retention (3 years or 1 year post-termination, whichever longer); annual reminders; fee-change notice 3–30/7–30 day windows per counsel summaries. Statute text: [BPC 17602](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=17602); [bill compare](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billCompareClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB2863); [Cooley 4 Jun 2025](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2025/2025-06-04-california-automatic-renewal-law-amendments-take-effect-on-july-1-2025) |
| **Official / cited affected** | Any business offering automatic renewal / continuous service (incl. free-to-pay) to CA consumers. No official “micro SaaS” census; practical buyers = indie subscription merchants on Stripe/Shopify Subscriptions. |
| **Recurring workflow** | Capture/store affirmative consent artifacts → annual reminder sends → cancel-path QA → fee-change notices → audit log. Workaround: Notion checklists + email templates + Stripe Dashboard manual cancels. |
| **Stakes** | Unlawful practices under ARL; private AG/class actions historically active in CA subscription space; restitution / civil penalties (counsel alerts). Federal Click-to-Cancel vacated — **CA remains binding**. |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | Mid-market billing: **Chargebee Retention**, Recurly, ProsperStack (enterprise). Chargebee blog on CA cancel flows (older 2022 ARL) [chargebee.com/blog](https://www.chargebee.com/blog/6-steps-to-california-friendly-online-cancel-flow/). Chrome/Shopify micro apps for **consent vault + annual reminder + cancel audit** at $20–80/mo appear underdeveloped vs billing platforms. |
| **Unmet gap** | Stripe Billing / Shopify Subscriptions plugin that (1) stores consent proofs, (2) fires annual reminders, (3) lint-checks cancel UX for CA residents — without migrating off Stripe. |
| **Distribution** | Stripe App Marketplace; Shopify; Indie Hackers; SaaS Twitter/X; CA AG press releases. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | State statute; ~30 states have ARLs (patchwork). Federal rulemaking restarted 2026 could eventually **preempt or harmonize** — timing uncertain post-vacatur. |

[Commons Library CBP-10669](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10669/); [EPLS 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/english-private-landlord-survey-2024-main-report/english-private-landlord-survey-2024-main-report). **Above** preferred 10k–500k; addressable paid SaaS = self-managing landlords (EPLS: 45% own one property; ~513k direct TDP landlords in survey frame). |

### 8) UK (England) — Simpler Recycling micro-firm workplace duty (deadline 31 Mar 2027)

| Field | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| **Norm / date** | Environment Act 2021 / Separation of Waste (England) Regulations; workplace separation of dry recyclables + **food waste** mandatory from **31 Mar 2025** for workplaces with **≥10 FTE**; **micro-firms (<10 FTE)** deadline **31 Mar 2027**. [GOV.UK guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/simpler-recycling-workplace-recycling-in-england); [Let’s Recycle FAQ v2.2 May 2025 PDF](https://www.letsrecycle.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Simpler-Recycling-FAQs-v2.2-May-259888.pdf) |
| **Official / cited affected** | All England workplaces. UK micro businesses **~5.4M** (0–9 employees) nationally ([DBT BPE / Commons SN06152](https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/SN06152/SN06152.pdf)) — **far above** 10k–500k; product must niche (e.g. hospitality micro sites generating food waste). |
| **Recurring workflow** | Contract separated collections → keep waste transfer notes → staff process → prove compliance on EA notice. Workaround: single general bin + email quotes from haulers. |
| **Stakes** | Environment Agency **compliance notices**, then escalating civil sanctions / prosecution for persistent breach (GOV.UK guidance). |
| **Competitors / prices / funding** | Waste majors **Veolia** etc. sell collection services [veolia.co.uk](https://www.veolia.co.uk/services/simpler-recycling); local brokers. **Software** for micro compliance proof (contracts, WTNs, reminder calendar) is thin vs hauler CRM. |
| **Unmet gap** | £20–40/mo “micro hospitality recycling compliance” (bin schedule + document vault + EA-notice response pack) sold via accountancy / local chamber — not another waste truck. |
| **Distribution** | Local councils; EA communications; hospitality associations; Bookkeep/Xero partner directories; Google Local Services. |
| **Sunset / preemption** | England-specific; Scotland/Wales already had food-waste regimes. Political rollback of micro-firm deadline possible but not evidenced as of research date. |
