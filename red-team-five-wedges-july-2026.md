### Why existing products are under-used (observed substitutes)

1. **Free official PDF** satisfies legal minimum (EPA sample checklist).
2. **One-time $50–75** cloud PDF packs undercut monthly SaaS.
3. Mecherath at **$399/mo** is full contractor suite price — overkill for RRP-only.
4. QuoteIQ/Jobber/CompanyCam win as **general FSM**; RRP is a checklist feature, not a must-buy SKU.
5. Many firms remain non-certified / informal (historical OIG under-adoption vs estimate) → low software willingness among non-compliers.

### Realistic SOM (clients, not TAM)

- Addressable certified firms: order **~10^5** historically certified (proxy), current live total **unknown**.
- Firms 1–20 that (a) do frequent pre-1978 work, (b) fear audits, (c) will pay >$20–50/mo for RRP-only after free PDF exists: **plausible obtainable ~200–2,000** paying seats over several years if product is RRP-narrow; higher only if bundled into FSM they already need.
- Not: “all 200k renovators.”

### Kill verdict: **narrow gap**

Facts: obligation is real and per-job; free EPA form + $75 organizers + FSM checklists already cover the compliance surface; dedicated RRP SaaS has existed (Mecherath claim “first and only”) without category dominance evidence. Gap is thin: mobile UX / multi-crew timestamps / retention, not absence of solution.

### Why existing products aren’t used

1. **Word is the court-familiar artifact**; many platforms export to Word anyway.
2. New AI report tools face **judicial/professional chill** (2025 UK/US incidents).
3. Directories solve **marketing**, not drafting.
4. ExpertPractice solves **billing + Rule 26 list**, not deep opinion drafting.
5. Expert Genie Pro / Expert IQ / Ecsper still early (pilot / Summer 2026 launch) — category forming, not mature.
6. Experts fear discovery/audit of AI tooling; prefer low-tech.

### Realistic SOM

- US: **500–3,000** obtainable paying experts for practice/report tooling over several years among digitally willing; not all SEAK listees.
- UK: **100–400** obtainable among EWI-scale + Bond Solon-trained population before saturation with Expert Genie / Expert IQ.
- Dual-jurisdiction “one OS” harder due to CPR35 vs FRCP26 divergence.

### Kill verdict: **narrow gap → becoming crowded (2025–2026 launches)**

Facts: mandatory disclosure/report rules are real; Word dominates; **multiple purpose-built entrants** launched or launching 2025–2026 (ExpertPractice, Draft.expert, Ecsper, Expert IQ, Expert Genie Pro). Gap for “yet another” general expert-report OS is **narrowing fast**. Remaining niche gaps (discipline-specific, disclosure packs, UK CPR35 templates) are **narrow**, not empty ocean.
